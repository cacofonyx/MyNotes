# Senior Director Playbook: Platform/SRE Leadership at a Growth-Phase IGA Company

> **Context:** A Senior Director of Platform Engineering / SRE at a growth-phase identity security company (like Saviynt), transitioning from enterprise culture, stepping up from Senior Engineering Manager. Triple transition: new company, elevated role, different culture.

> **Purpose:** Not a book summary. A synthesized operating manual — drawing from High Output Management, The Manager's Path, The First 90 Days, DevOps Handbook, Implementing SLOs, Platform Engineering, and additional leadership thinking — rewritten as actionable guidance for this specific situation.

> **Philosophy:** These notes are written for someone who takes things at face value, prefers explicit frameworks over implicit intuition, and needs signal→interpretation guidance for organizational dynamics.

## How to Read This

- **Chapters are independent.** Read in order for full arc, or jump to what's urgent.
- **Each chapter has:** frameworks, anti-patterns, worked examples specific to IGA/platform context, and "signal → interpretation" blocks for organizational dynamics.
- **Sources are synthesized, not summarized.** The thinking is combined and rewritten for this specific situation. Original book notes remain in their own directories.
