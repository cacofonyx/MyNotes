# IGA Knowledge Guide: From Zero to Depth

> **Context:** A Platform Engineering / SRE leader joining Saviynt — a cloud-native Identity Governance & Administration company. No prior IGA domain experience. Need to understand the space deeply enough to lead platform decisions, speak credibly with product/engineering, and see where SRE/Platform thinking applies.

> **Purpose:** Self-study guide that builds IGA knowledge from scratch. Not a product manual — a domain understanding guide with Saviynt context woven in where relevant. Written for engineers and engineering leaders who think in systems, SLOs, and platforms.

> **Philosophy:** Each chapter is self-contained. No assumed background beyond general software engineering. Concepts introduced fresh with concrete scenarios. SRE/Platform lens applied throughout — because that's how you'll engage with this domain.

## Format Per Chapter

Each chapter includes:
- **Plain-language explanation** — no assumed IGA background
- **Real-world scenarios** — concrete examples, not abstract theory
- **Saviynt-specific angle** — where relevant to understanding the platform
- **SRE/Platform lens** — callout boxes connecting to reliability/platform thinking
- **Self-test questions** — "can you explain this to someone?"
- **Key terms** — glossary of chapter-specific terminology
