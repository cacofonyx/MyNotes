# Part II — Conclusion

Part II covered how to choose where to start a DevOps transformation: selecting value streams, understanding work, organizational design (Conway's Law), and integrating Operations into Development's daily work.

Part III moves to the technical practices of Flow — the specific implementations that enable fast movement from Development to Operations without causing chaos downstream.

## Key Resources

- *Team Topologies* (Skelton & Pais) — organizing business and technology teams for fast flow
- *Individual Contributors: From Holdouts to Holdups* whitepaper — working with change-resistant individuals
- *Expanding Pockets of Greatness* whitepaper — spreading DevOps horizontally across an organization
- Paula Thrasher, "Interactive Virtual Value Stream Mapping" (DevOps Enterprise Summit 2020) — running VSM workshops

> **[SRE Lens]** Part II's core lesson for SRE: you don't transform everything at once. Pick one value stream, map it, understand where the bottlenecks are (often handoffs between Dev and Ops), and improve that first. For SRE teams, the natural starting point is usually the service with the highest incident rate or the worst deployment experience — the pain is already visible and the team is already motivated to change.
